Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about becoming a food taster and using our platform.

How much can I earn as a food taster?

Earnings vary depending on the specific assignment, the time required, and the complexity of the feedback needed. We aim to connect you with opportunities that offer competitive hourly rates or project fees based on the nature of the task.

Do I need professional culinary experience?

Generally, no. Most brands are looking for honest feedback from everyday consumers to understand how their products perform in the real world. A sensitive palate and the ability to articulate your thoughts clearly are the most important qualifications.

Is this a full-time job?

Most opportunities found through our platform are part-time, freelance, or project-based. This offers great flexibility, allowing you to browse for roles that fit your existing schedule.

Are jobs guaranteed after I sign up?

No, signing up gives you access to browse potential listings, but it does not guarantee employment or specific assignments. Availability of roles depends on market demand and your location.
