Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the application process.

Is there a fee to apply?
No, applying for jobs through our platform is 100% free for job seekers. We partner directly with employers who cover the costs of recruitment.
How fast is the approval process?
Our "Live Application System" processes initial data instantly. Most candidates receive a status update or interview request within 24-48 hours of submission.
Are these remote or on-site jobs?
We have both! You can filter for "Remote," "Hybrid," or "On-site" positions. We currently have a high demand for Work-From-Home data entry and customer support roles.
Do I need prior experience?
Many of our entry-level positions (like Warehouse Associate or Data Entry) do not require prior experience. We also offer training resources for approved applicants.
